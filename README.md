# Tangerine Inc. Sales Performance & Revenue Insights (2024)

A structured Power BI sales performance report analysing Tangerine Inc.'s 2024 sales across salespeople, regions, products, customers, and payment methods. Built as part of my data analytics training with Vephla.

![Report preview](tangerine_sales_performance_dashboard.png)

## Objectives

- Measure sales performance by salesperson, region, product, and customer
- Identify key revenue drivers and payment trends
- Analyse demand patterns and revenue concentration
- Present findings through clear and interactive dashboard layouts

## Tools

- Power BI – data modelling, visualisation, and page navigation
- Figma – report layout and visual design

## Key Insights

1. **Coffee ($75,486) and Curry Sauce ($69,000)** generated the highest revenue among the products analysed.
2. **Clam Chowder recorded the highest quantity sold (1,744 units)** but generated only $16,829.60 in revenue.
3. Revenue was concentrated among a small group of customers, particularly Companies D, H, BB, and F.
4. Salesperson performance was uneven, with **Nancy Freehafer** significantly ahead of the other salespeople.
5. Revenue peaked in **Q4**, with the **North and East regions** recording the strongest performance.

## Recommendations

- Scale high-performing products through targeted stock planning and promotional activities.
- Review pricing and revenue contribution for high-volume, lower-revenue products.
- Diversify the customer base to reduce customer concentration risk.
- Share best practices from top-performing salespeople across the wider sales team.
- Prepare inventory and sales strategies ahead of the Q4 peak period.
